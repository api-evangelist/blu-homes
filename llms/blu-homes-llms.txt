# Dvele

> Dvele is a US-based manufacturer of high-performance, all-electric prefab modular homes built for the climate era. Founded in 2018, Dvele homes exceed Passive House standards, are net-positive in energy, and are produced through advanced off-site manufacturing to be healthier, more sustainable, and more affordable than conventional construction.

Dvele (from the Norwegian word for "dwell") engineers homes around health, energy independence, and longevity. Core proprietary systems include Self-Powered™ (solar-plus-battery energy independence that keeps critical systems running during grid outages) and Dvele IQ™ (a home operating system that monitors air and water quality, humidity, energy use, and VOC levels). The company has delivered over 270 modules. Note: all canonical URLs use the www subdomain (https://www.dvele.com).

## Benefits and technology

- [Self-Powered™](https://www.dvele.com/benefit/self-powered): Solar and battery system for clean, renewable energy and grid-outage resilience.
- [Dvele IQ™](https://www.dvele.com/benefit/dvele-iq): The home's operating system, collecting millions of data points monthly to monitor home and occupant health.
- [Environmental Impact](https://www.dvele.com/benefit/environmental-impact): Net-positive energy and reduced embodied carbon in production.
- [Health-Centric](https://www.dvele.com/benefit/health-centric): Design focused on clean air, clean water, and occupant well-being.
- [Advanced Manufacturing](https://www.dvele.com/benefit/advanced-manufacturing): Next-generation building science and off-site production techniques.
